🎨 Design Principles

  • Canvas as Sacred Space: Mobile writing space should remain clean with minimal UI clutter
  • 1-2 Tap Maximum: Minimize friction to start writing with intelligent defaults
  • Smart Detection: Leverage article title + language from URL (simulating Wikidata lookup)
  • Template-based: Show fillable templates with placeholders, not examples to study
  • Mobile-first: All interactions optimized for touch and small screens
